ANSWERS THAT RECOMMEND NOTHING
   Q1 (kind of business) and Q2 (how many people) set the business type and
   size used for the hours estimate. "Something else", "Under 5" and
   "Over 50" have no size or type on record.
   Q8 only changes the payoff line.
   Q9 only changes the closing line.
   If nothing is triggered the result says:
   "Nothing in your answers points to work being done twice, late or by hand."
   "Our recommendation is to buy nothing yet."
