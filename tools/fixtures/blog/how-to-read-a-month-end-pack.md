A month-end pack is read in order: the result, then what moved it, then what to do. Most packs are built the other way round, which is why they take days to assemble and minutes to ignore.

## Start with the one number

The first page should hold one figure and the date it was true. If the reader has to turn a page to find out whether the month was good, the pack is in the wrong order.

## Then the three things that moved it

- Revenue against the same month last year.
- Gross margin by job or by product line.
- Cash in the bank against what is owed this month.

### Where the figures come from

Every figure in a pack comes from a system the business already runs. Our [reporting and dashboards](/services/reporting-and-dashboards) work builds the pack from those systems, so nobody types it.
